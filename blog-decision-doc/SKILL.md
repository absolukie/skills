# Blog Decision Doc Skill

## Purpose
Publish blog posts directly. Luke granted standing permission (2026-10-08, reaffirmed 2026-10-10) to publish blogs and visualizers straight to the live site without a draft review or decision doc. He iterates after publishing. The decision doc workflow below is OPT-IN only: build one only when he explicitly asks for "blog decision doc", "run it through the decision doc", or "vet this post". Otherwise, write the post, QA it, and ship it.

## Workflow
1. Get the draft (URL or full text) and read it completely.
2. Break it into paragraph blocks in order. For each paragraph, draft 2–3 genuine wording alternatives in Luke's voice (plain, direct, verdict-first, no AI-feel) — tighter cuts, sharper angles, not trivial rewordings. For quotes: keep / shorten / cut-or-paraphrase options. For the title: current plus 3 fresh alternatives.
3. Build the decision doc as its own top-level folder on the projects router: `sites/<slug>/index.html` in `absolukie/lukezhang-si`, slug added to `SITES` in `worker.js`, entry added to `sites/design-docs/docs.json` so it appears in the design docs section. Back link points to `../design-docs/`. Push via gh_push.py, verify the live URL.
4. Page structure: one card per paragraph in reading order — section kicker, the CURRENT text shown, the alternative wordings as tappable option rows (current = "Keep as is"), plus a "My wording" notes field per paragraph for his own rewrite. End with a final-call card (keep live / revise / unpublish) and an Other notes field. Everything autosaved under a post-specific storageKey; sticky footer copies the full decision summary with a toast. By the last card he has read the whole post.

## Output Contract
- One decision doc per post: a paragraph-by-paragraph walkthrough in reading order, 2–3 real wording alternatives per paragraph, "My wording" field on each, final call at the end. No verdict-question checklists — he reads the whole post through the doc.

## Operating Rules
1. Follow the `decision-board` skill for all board mechanics: autosave, 16px+ inputs, `touch-action: manipulation`, big tap targets, iPhone one-handed, sticky viewport-fixed copy footer with safe-area padding, toast on copy, per-question notes (never one shared box).
2. Be honest in the pre-fill — flag real issues, don't rubber-stamp. If the verdict is buried or the voice is off, say so.
3. When he finishes a decision doc, report verdict-first: his calls, his notes, then what happens next. Save durable outcomes to memory.
4. **Every post is a visualizer, split into blogs and primers.** (Stated 2026-10-07: "make all blogs visualizers of course." Restated 2026-10-09: "When I reference blog I always mean it should be a visualizer too." Split 2026-10-10: personal = blogs, external = primers. He rotates the external name between "primers" and "explainers", never both at once.) Two buckets:
   - **Blog** = personal. About things HE did. His builds, projects, experiences. Lives at `/blog/`.
   - **Primer** (aka explainer) = external. About what OTHERS did. Concepts, papers, other people's work. Lives at `/primers/`.
   Both are visualizers, never text-only. The word "blog" from him means a personal post WITH an interactive visualizer. "Primer"/"explainer" means an external post WITH an interactive visualizer. Never ship text-only and then ask whether he wants a visualizer. Every post ships with interactive visualizers, not just text: real reference images where they exist (embedded with credit; no contemporary portrait of Sun Tzu exists, so a Qing-era depiction with an honest caption), and interactive elements where motion or comparison is the point. Side-by-side comparisons are elite. Made-for-the-post visuals beat stock art. Never stock, never forced.
5. **Tag every post from one consistent taxonomy.** (Stated 2026-10-07: "just have good tags for my blog posts." Rebuilt 2026-10-09: one-off tags merged into broader homes.) Two tags per post, drawn from the ten main categories, in this order: ai, engineering, llms, math, infrastructure, culture, tools, business, faith, life. A third bucket, "other", holds design, language, science for the rare post that fits none of the ten. When a topic feels too specific for its own tag, fold it into the nearest broad home instead of minting a new one. The 2026-10-09 merge is the reference: apis, python, cli, oauth, patterns, security, testing, webgl, shaders went to engineering; kubernetes, networking, vpn, optimization went to infrastructure; memes and x went to culture; lean went to math; interviews went to business; biology and neuroscience became science; app design and penpot became design; etymology folded into language. Normalize on write: "machine learning" becomes ai, "ai agents" becomes ai. Never tag a post "interactive"; every post is interactive, so the tag says nothing.
6. **Cross-link the network.** (Stated 2026-10-07: "link to other blogs when you can.") Every visual explainer ends with a Keep exploring section: 3-5 link cards to his related posts and visualizers (other blog posts, Model Atlas, standalone visual sites), each with a one-line gloss of what it adds. The goal is a web of posts that build on each other, so reading one pulls him deeper into the next.
7. **LaTeX is available in every post.** (Stated 2026-10-07: "render latex in my blogs.") KaTeX 0.16.11 is vendored at `blog/assets/katex/` in `absolukie/lukezhang-si` (zero third-party requests, per his self-hosting rule). Every post includes the snippet: `<link rel="stylesheet" href="/blog/assets/katex/katex.min.css">` plus `katex.min.js` and `contrib/auto-render.min.js` with `renderMathInElement(document.body)` and explicit `$`/`$$` delimiters (KaTeX 0.16's auto-render defaults no longer include single-`$`, so always pass delimiters explicitly; same for any dynamic injection). Write formulas as `$...$` inline and `$$...$$` display. Literal dollar amounts go in `<span class="nokatex">$1</span>` with `ignoredClasses: ["nokatex"]` in the auto-render config (backslash-escaping renders a visible backslash in 0.16, do not use `\$`). Draft previews under `sites/<slug>/` use a per-folder `katex/` copy with relative paths, since the preview domain cannot reach `/blog/assets/`; switch to `/blog/assets/katex/` paths when the post ships to `blog/<slug>/`.

8. **Code snippets get syntax highlighting.** (Stated 2026-10-08: "for my code snippets, i want there to be syntax highlighting pls!") Every code block in a post gets syntax highlighting, never a plain monochrome block. Use the vendored highlight.js at `/blog/assets/highlight.min.js` (v11.9.0, Python + JS bundled, self-hosted so zero third-party requests) with the warm theme at `/blog/assets/highlight-theme.css`. Markup: `<pre><code class="language-python">` (or `language-javascript`), plus `<script>hljs.highlightAll();</script>` before `</body>`. Never hand-roll a tokenizer (2026-10-08: his "steal the library" principle). Palette lives in the theme file: keywords `#e8a15c` (amber), strings `#a3c98a` (soft green), comments `#8a7a68` (muted warm gray), numbers `#7fc4c9` (teal), builtins/functions `#f0a8c8` (pink).
9. **Research uses the most modern papers available.** (Stated 2026-10-09.) When a post needs research, find the latest peer-reviewed papers on the topic, not the most-cited classics. Check publication dates; a 2025 paper beats a 2018 paper on the same finding. Prefer primary sources (the paper itself, official data) over news summaries. If newer research updates or contradicts an older finding, the newer one wins and the post says so. Every factual claim gets its exact source link.
10. **Re-read the visualizers skill before building.** (Stated 2026-10-10.) Every post is a visualizer, so the shared patterns in `~/workspace/skills/visualizers/SKILL.md` apply to blogs too: the five visualizer patterns, the cross-pattern rules (click-per-step steppers, arrow anchoring, 16px inputs, `touch-action:manipulation`, bespoke icons, source citations), and the QA checklist. Re-read it before every build, same as the primer skill requires.
11. **New posts ship with verification.** (Stated 2026-10-12.) Every new blog post gets the `create-verification-skill` treatment for its visualizer before it is called done: feature map from the reader's point of view, a harness driving the real UI, a saved report, results in the ship report. When a decision doc is built for a post, it notes the verification status. Existing posts are not backfilled unless he asks.
