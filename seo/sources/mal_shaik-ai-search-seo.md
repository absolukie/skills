## Summary

Claims ElevenLabs "demolished their competition in AI search" and reverse-engineers
their four-part content strategy:

1. **One landing page per language** — "french text to speech", "arabic text to
   speech", "hindi text to speech", "bengali text to speech", etc. Dozens of
   pages, each targeting a different query/market.
2. **Separate content verticals per product** — beyond TTS: dubbing, voice
   cloning, speech-to-text, voice isolator, AI agents.
3. **Answer-first feature pages** — pages like "what is AI dubbing" / "how does
   voice cloning work" answer the question before pitching, making ElevenLabs
   the natural next step.
4. **Language x feature matrix** — every language times every feature gets its
   own landing page, compounding into hundreds of indexed pages from one strategy.

Closes with a CTA: comment "audit" for a company deep-audit.
