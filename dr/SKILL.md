# DR — Design Review

A DR reviews one work that needs Luke's decisions. Kit walks it piece by piece; Luke taps his calls.

## Scope: one DR, one goal
- **DRs are how a workstream iterates (Luke, 2026-10-19, in his words: "I basically use DRs for iterating on the same workstream over and over until final product. Even the final product will be a DR itself too").** A DR is never a one-shot review: the same subject gets pass after pass, each pass building the work further from the last rulings, until the final product exists, and the final product is itself delivered as a DR (see the delivery rule in the Output contract). A batch of separate finished things never shares one delivery board: each subject gets its own delivery DR, even when the batch was produced together (Luke, 2026-10-19, correcting a five-video combined delivery board).
- One goal per board, stated on the intro step in one sentence. If it takes two sentences, it is two DRs. (Set 2026-10-17.)
- Never bundle unrelated calls. A second goal that surfaces becomes its own DR.
- Subjects (a channel, an app, a post) accumulate small focused DRs over time, never one growing mega-board. Example, Your Soup Is Typing: separate DRs for its background image, its video tool's features, Monday's video, and future video ideas.
- Building a DR includes categorizing it (see Deployment). An uncategorized board is unfinished.

## Trigger
"DR this", "run a DR", "design review", "put this through a DR", "review this"; any post, site, design, copy, or plan nearing a ship, revise, or kill call.

## Workflow
1. Read the source in full. Every word and every diagram goes on the board, in original order, nothing summarized away.
2. Break it into blocks: one card per paragraph, section, screen, or decision point, in order.
3. Pre-vet each block: what is strong, what is weak, what needs his call. Write 2 to 3 genuine alternatives per block, never filler.
4. Build the board per the contract below.
5. Deploy, verify live, send the link.
6. On submit, report verdict-first: his picks, his notes, what happens next. Save durable decisions to memory.
7. Read his answers from `GET /api/decision/responses?slug=X&key=DD_READ_KEY` on the private router (private.lukezhang.si; the same endpoint also serves on projects.lukezhang.si. The key is in the public worker.js; obscurity-level, fine for low-sensitivity picks). Dedupe repeat submits; report picks, then notes, then what changes.

## Privacy: gated, never anonymized
Boards and every other private page sit behind ONE Cloudflare Access app covering the whole hostname private.lukezhang.si (app "Private (private.lukezhang.si)", id 8399c5bf-66a4-4375-8dbb-4cf8750231b0; absolukie@gmail.com only, one-time PIN, 24-hour sessions), so content goes on the board exactly as written, names included; never redact. There is no per-board gate step anymore: the whole-host app gates every path on private.lukezhang.si automatically, and the old per-path apps (DR Boards, DR Boards 2, Morning Mercy, and the rest) are legacy rollback only, never to be extended. Coverage inventory: ~/workspace/design-docs-gate/COVERAGE.md. /api/decision/* stays ungated on purpose and serves on both hostnames: boards POST submits there, and reads are key-protected.

## Output contract
- Block = card, in order: content verbatim, 2 to 3 tappable alternatives, an explicit question ("Your call: ..."), a Kit's take note, a notes field.
- Title pick (or the equivalent framing call) first.
- **Question first, answers answer it (Luke, 2026-10-19, from the primer-cut board).** The decision is phrased as a direct question and rendered as the largest, most prominent text on the step. Every option is worded as a direct answer to that exact question, never a topic label or a proposal title. Context and evidence sit around the question: Kit's take small under it, the full source text below the options and notes, demoted. Never a wall of context above the answers with the question trailing underneath; if Luke has to hunt for what is being asked, the block is wrong.
- **Context above the question (Luke, 2026-10-21, on gif-pixel-nocturne-2: "Note: In the future can you put the context above the question for all my DRs thanks. Save to my DR skill").** On every block, the context and evidence sit ABOVE the question, so he reads the context first, then the question, then the options and notes. This supersedes the placement part of the question first rule above: the question stays a direct question and the options still answer it directly, but context no longer sits below the options.
- **Final step always offers another pass, and a pass delivers multiple results (Luke, 2026-10-19, vision board plus his hamster lock-in: "even when I'm happy I want another DR anyways for final product to get multiple results of what I asked for... the point of DRs is to just keep doing DR passes over and over and over").** Every DR's final call includes the option "Read all my answers and do another DR pass on them." **The follow-up pass is the default after ANY submit, even a complete one (Luke, 2026-10-19): Kit reads the answers and builds the next DR pass without waiting to be asked, because the final product itself is delivered and reviewed through DR passes too. The loop stops only when Luke explicitly says no more passes, or picks park or kill; silence never stops it.** A follow-up pass is not just new questions: where the work produces a thing, the pass BUILDS it and presents multiple finished results of what he asked for (variants of the render, the cut, the draft), not one. A locked, happy answer does not end the loop either; the standing move is build the thing AND send another DR with multiple results of it. Settled answers become inputs, open threads and notes become the new questions.
- **The title pick goes last, or not at all (Luke, 2026-10-19, on the vision board's title question).** He cannot name a thing before he knows what is in it, and asking him to name the DR itself is a bad question ("not clear what a board is"). The framing or title call sits at the end, after the substance and before the final call, and is skipped entirely when the work already carries a working title. This replaces "title pick first" everywhere except the meat-first exception, which already skips it.
- **Option labels are the answer only (Luke, 2026-10-19).** No answer-plus-tagline options: an option's label is the answer itself, clean. Supporting color, caveats, and color commentary belong in the take or the context block, never appended to the option text.
- **The intro is short (Luke, 2026-10-19, screenshot of the vision board: "the first page is too wordy, needs to be clear and shorter").** The intro step carries the one-sentence goal and at most two more short lines: what is on the board in one line, and anything he must know before starting (for a follow-up round, his own commissioning notes verbatim). No tour of the questions, no paragraph about where the material came from, no reassurance copy. If the intro needs a scroll, it is wrong; provenance and method belong in the context blocks of the questions they support.
- **Final products are delivered in a DR, always (Luke, 2026-10-19, on the hamster video file sent as a chat attachment: "I don't want you to give it to me like this when I say let's run it. I always want final product in a DR still").** When work produces a finished thing (a video, a render, a draft), the deliverable is a DR board (or the next delivery pass of its board) with the result playable or readable inside it and the decision options around it. Never deliver a finished product as a bare file attachment in chat, even when he says build it or run it: building on his word still ends in a board, and chat carries only the link and the take.
- **Kit's take stays short and clear (Luke, 2026-10-19, from the primer-skill-rebuild board: "the Kit's take was really hard to read... I never read any because they were so dense").**
- **Tag the recommended option (Luke, 2026-10-19, on the skill-tree-mastery board).** The option Kit recommends carries a visible "Recommended" tag ON the option itself, so the recommendation is findable without reading prose. The tag sits beside the label, never appended into the answer text (labels stay the answer only). Applies wherever Kit has a genuine lean; no lean, no tag. The take is at most two short sentences: the recommendation first, then the one reason that matters. No hedging chains, no evidence dumps (evidence belongs in the context block below). If a take needs a third sentence, the question or the options are carrying too little and should be fixed instead.
- Title pick (or the equivalent framing call) LAST, per the title-last rule above; it is skipped when the work has a working title. EXCEPTION (Luke, 2026-10-19, on the hamster DR): when the DR's subject is a generated work itself, above all a video, skip the framing calls and lead with the meat: the first question is the work and its options, playable. A title pick on a video DR is a pointless question; context is fine around the meat, never before it.
- Title-ideas step on video DRs (Luke, 2026-10-19, hamster delivery: "generate me a bunch of ideas for the title too in these video DRs. Can do it near the end once the video is locked"). Once a video is locked, the board (or its delivery pass) offers a BUNCH of title ideas near the end, not three: grouped by style, including lowercase question titles and emoji-bearing YouTube Shorts styles. His incumbent pick, if he already named one, rides as an option. A title-ideas generator tool lives in the Soup Pantry tools for reuse across videos.
- **Real artifacts, no stand-ins.** (Set 2026-10-17: "I want the actual prototypes not shortcuts.") Every option is the thing itself: videos embedded and playable (full render, or a short true preview render if unproduced); images at full size; designs as mockups or working prototypes; features as working mini-demos where possible; text options in full. If an artifact cannot be produced or embedded, the block says so, gives the reason, and links the live thing. A description never poses as the artifact.
- **Every video carries a download link (Luke, 2026-10-19).** Every video on a board carries a download link under it: an anchor whose href is the same file URL the video plays with "?download=1" appended (for example media/final.mp4?download=1), with the download attribute, labeled "Download video" (template-enforced in gen_board.py). The media route answers a ?download=1 request with a Content-Disposition attachment response so iPhone Safari offers a save instead of only playing inline; on repo-served boards the parameter is harmless and ignored.
- Step-through, not scroll: one question per screen. Intro, questions in order, Other notes, Final call. Sticky stepbar: progress bar, "Question N of M · K left" counter, Back and Next. Next never moves; Back is disabled, never hidden, on step 0. Dots under the buttons: green when answered, ring on the current one, tap to jump. The final step's Next is Submit. Arrow keys navigate, ignored while typing. Scroll to top on step change. Selecting an option inside a step updates in place and never scrolls the page; the scroll reset belongs to step changes only.
- Autosave: instant and invisible to localStorage under a per-DR key. The server draft (`POST /api/decision/autosave`) and the footer pill debounce together after about 1.2s idle: the pill never flickers per keystroke; it shows "Saving…" (pulsing amber), then "Autosaved h:mm" (green).
- Sticky footer: autosave pill, Copy answers (toast "✓ Answers copied"), Submit (spinner and "Submitting…" while the POST is in flight, then restores and toasts). Submit posts {slug, picks, notes, final, otherNotes} to `POST /api/decision/submit`.
- Mobile-first: inputs 16px minimum, `touch-action: manipulation` on tap targets, safe-area footer padding, back link to the DR overview.
- No em dashes in any copy, ever. Use commas, colons, or periods.

## Board status (overview)
- **Completed:** Luke has submitted, even if some questions are unanswered.
- **Partially complete:** not submitted, but he has started answering one or more questions (a server draft from autosave, or a draft still on his device).
- **Open:** not submitted and nothing answered.
The overview derives these from submissions and drafts on the decision API; there is no separate done flag.

## Operating rules
1. Luke makes every final call. Kit pre-vets, never decides.
2. Creative work shows multiple distinct options, never one take as the answer.
3. Every decision is tappable. Never "reply with option B".
4. Board first, explanation second. The board is the whole interface: no Google Docs, no email threads.
5. Notes are per-block, under the options, on every block, with a Clear notes button (right-aligned, muted); clearing autosaves and offers Undo for 5 seconds. Other notes gets the same.
6. Options toggle: tapping a picked option deselects it. Never trap him in a choice.
7. Summaries lead with the verdict, reasons after.

## Deployment
Create `sites/<slug>/index.html` in absolukie/lukezhang-si; add the slug to `SITES` and to `PRIVATE_SITES` in worker.js (fetch it fresh from the GitHub API first; never edit a stale copy) and an entry in `sites/design-docs/docs.json` (slug, title, subtitle, date, question count, storageKey, category). That registry is what lists the board on the DR overview; the design-docs page itself is retired and redirects to the overview. The category comes from a fixed set (YouTube, Games, Apps, Business, Writing, Faith, Personal, Systems); pick the closest fit. Add a new category only when none fits, and note the addition in the ship report. Back link: `../dr-overview/` (the DR overview, per Luke's 2026-10-18 ruling). Push via `~/workspace/skills/github/bin/gh_push.py`. Verify the live URL returns 200 and the mechanics work. No per-board gate step exists anymore: the board lives at `https://private.lukezhang.si/<slug>/` under the whole-host Access app, and its slug must also be added to `PRIVATE_SITES` in worker.js (that list is what serves the slug on the private host and 301-redirects the old projects.lukezhang.si URL to it). Before sending the link, verify an unauthenticated fetch of the private URL returns 302 to the Access login with zero page bytes, and that the old projects URL returns 301 to the private host. Never send a board ungated.
Board videos are compressed per the deploy flow in ~/workspace/drbuild/deploy_board.py and served from R2 at /media/<slug>/ on the private host, never embedded in the repo.
